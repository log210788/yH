2. Navigating Responsibility (Workplace Subtext)

Read each variation with strong stress on the highlighted word. Click button to reveal subtext.

Full Sentence & Target Stress Hidden Workplace Subtext
"I didn't tell him to cancel the project."
"Someone else gave that order—don't look at me!"
"I DIDN'T tell him to cancel the project."
"Strong, direct denial of a false accusation."
"I didn't TELL him to cancel the project."
"I implied or suggested it, but never ordered it."
"I didn't tell HIM to cancel the project."
"I told the project lead or manager, not him."
"I didn't tell him to CANCEL the project."
"I said pause or postpone, not kill it completely!"
"I didn't tell him to cancel the PROJECT."
"I said cancel today's meeting, not the whole project!"

3. Word Stress Diagnostic (Noun vs. Verb)

Ask student to mark 1st syllable (Noun) or 2nd syllable (Verb) stress.

• "Can you reCORD this meeting?" / "Break the RECord."
• "It's a nice PREsent." / "Please preSENT your work."
• "We signed a new CONtract." / "Metals conTRACT in cold."

4. Student Presentation (60 Seconds)

Have the student select ONE option below and speak for 1 minute off the cuff.

Option A (Workplace): Briefly explain a project or task you recently completed.
Option B (Opinion): Pitch your favourite coffee shop, restaurant, or city.
Option C (Story): Tell a short story about a time something went wrong.
Evaluation Criteria Teacher Live Diagnostic Notes
Content Word Punching Did they clearly boost nouns, main verbs, and adjectives?
Rhythm & Reduction Did function words shrink to weak forms or stay equal length?
