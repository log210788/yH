## 1. The 32-Term Executive Leadership, Retail & Crisis Precision Matrix

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Core Rule for All 12 Exercises**: Elementary conversational habits (*"we did"*, *"useless things"*, *"put more items"*, *"bad things happen"*, *"subordinates are grown up"*, *"mark/bottom/cable"*) are **off-limits**. Use this matrix to upgrade into decisive C-suite English.

### A. Executive Agency, Retail Merchandising & Upward Management (From Oct 21 Session)
| Conversational / L1 Slip (Avoid) | Executive C1/C2 Upgrade | Practical Meaning & Nuance | 🇯🇵 日本語の解説 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *"We looked at / We did"* | **I spearheaded / I championed** | Acting as the primary driving force of an initiative. | 陣頭指揮を執った・主導した |
| *"My job was to..."* | **My primary mandate was to...** | An authoritative executive mission or directive. | 最重要ミッション・委任事項 |
| *"Split resources to useless things"* | **Dilute operational bandwidth across low-yield channels** | Wasting team capacity on low-return activities. | 低収益チャネルに運用リソースを分散させる |
| *"Put more items for good brands"* | **Onboard high-margin SKUs for top-performing brand partners** | Expanding retail catalog with profitable merchandise. | 主力ブランドの高収益SKU（商品）を新規導入する |
| *"Sales driving factors"* | **Primary growth levers / Revenue catalysts** | Core commercial drivers that accelerate top-line revenue. | 売上を牽引する中核ドライバー |
| *"Once bad things happen, report"* | **Escalate operational bottlenecks immediately with mitigation options** | Proactively flagging risks alongside ready-to-execute solutions. | 業務上のボトルネックを緩和策と共に即座にエスカレーションする |
| *"What the boss wants to know"* | **Anticipate strategic priorities & latent risks** | Foreseeing what senior leadership loses sleep over (The 70/30 Rule). | 経営陣の戦略的優先事項と潜在リスクを先回りして察知する |
| *"Information gap with the boss"* | **Asymmetrical communication / Information asymmetry** | Imbalance where frontline details and executive priorities don't match. | 情報の非対称性・非対称なコミュニケーション |

### B. Scaling 100+ Teams, Span of Control & Governance
| Conversational / L1 Slip (Avoid) | Executive C1/C2 Upgrade | Practical Meaning & Nuance | 🇯🇵 日本語の解説 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *"See my subordinates grown up"* | **Foster professional development / Cultivate talent** | Helping team members advance professionally (*"grow up"* = children only!). | 部下の専門的成長を促進する・人材を育成する |
| *"Give them freedom to do it"* | **Grant high-trust self-autonomy** | Empowering direct reports to execute without micromanagement. | 高い信頼に基づく自律性（裁量権）を付与する |
| *"Manage 4–5 leaders over 100 staff"* | **Optimize my span of control across a tiered hierarchy** | Structuring reporting lines so leadership scales efficiently. | 階層型組織において統率スパン（管理対象人数）を最適化する |
| *"Check halfway so we don't fail"* | **Install a half-goal circuit breaker** | A pre-engineered midpoint audit to catch flaws before deadline. | 中間監査ポイント（ハーフゴール・サーキットブレーカー）を設ける |
| *"Fighting / angry team culture"* | **Combative / Contentious dynamics** | Overly aggressive internal friction that harms collaboration. | 対立的で刺々しい（好戦的な）チーム環境 |
| *"Promote people who do good work"* | **Operate a strict meritocracy (rely on merit)** | Advancing personnel purely based on quantifiable output. | 厳格な成果主義・実力主義を貫く |
| *"Lose easy profit"* | **Leave money on the table** | Failing to capture commercial margin that buyers were willing to pay. | 取れるはずの利益を取りこぼす |
| *"Got scolded badly by the boss"* | **Got chewed out / Formally reprimanded** | Receiving a severe reprimand for missing standards. | 大目玉を食らった・厳しく叱責された |

### C. High-Pressure Crisis Precision & Closed-Loop Communication (From Oct 23 Session)
| Conversational / L1 Slip (Avoid) | Executive C1/C2 Upgrade | Practical Meaning & Nuance | 🇯🇵 日本語の解説 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *"Push the white bottom"* | **Press and hold the button** vs. **Tap and release** | Distinguishing continuous hold from an instant click (*button*, not *bottom*!). | ボタンを長押しする vs. 押してすぐ離す |
| *"The four-color diagram / mark"* | **The diamond (rhombus) keypad / Indicator symbol** | Precise geometric and interface terminology. | ひし形（菱形）キーパッド・インジケーター記号 |
| *"Four cables / pipelines"* | **Four adjacent wires / Connector leads** | Exact hardware/interface description. | 隣接する4本のワイヤー（配線） |
| *"Right on top / center below"* | **Top-right quadrant / Bottom-center module** | Crisp 2D grid coordinates (`Row 1, Column 3`). | 右上区画・中央下モジュール |
| *"Is the last number 1, 3, 5?"* | **Does the serial number terminate in an odd digit?** | *Odd digits* (`1, 3, 5, 7, 9`) vs. *Even digits* (`0, 2, 4, 6, 8`). | シリアル番号の末尾は奇数（odd）か偶数（even）か |
| *"Wait, what did you say?"* | **Closed-loop readback: "Confirming—do we hold or deploy?"** | Repeating back critical instructions before executing under pressure. | 復唱確認（クローズドループ確認） |
| *"We got a claim / bad cospa"* | **Escalated a grievance / Unsustainable ROI** | Eliminating Japanese *Wasei-Eigo* in global meetings. | 苦情がエスカレーションされた・採算（ROI）が合わない |
| *"If you give us X, maybe we do Y"* | **Provided that you meet us on X, we are prepared to concede Y** | High-authority conditional trade-off framing. | Xの条件をのんでいただけるなら、Yを譲歩する用意がある |
