## 1. The 30-Term Marketing & Commercial Upgrade Matrix

> **Core Rule**: In our marketing and business simulations, elementary conversational words (*make, get, good, bad, big problem, change, cheap*) are **off-limits**. Use this matrix to upgrade your language into decisive commercial English.

### A. Positioning, Branding & Copywriting
| Basic Phrase (Avoid) | Executive Marketing Upgrade | How to Use It in a Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Good points of our product | **Value proposition / USP** | *"Our core **value proposition** is cutting manual reporting time by 80%."* |
| Customer problem / headache | **Pain point / Friction** | *"A 12-field registration form creates too much **friction** for mobile users."* |
| Group of people we sell to | **Target persona / Demographic** | *"This campaign is tailored specifically for our enterprise **target persona**."* |
| The button at the bottom | **Call-to-Action (CTA)** | *"Let's replace our vague button with a benefit-driven **call-to-action**."* |
| Company image ("Image-up") | **Brand equity / Credibility** | *"Slashing prices by 30% will permanently **erode our brand equity**."* |
| Change who we sell to | **Reposition / Pivot** | *"We decided to **reposition** the app from B2C students to corporate HR teams."* |
| Make us look different | **Differentiate / Stand out** | *"How do we **differentiate** our offering in such a crowded market?"* |
| Customer reviews / logos | **Social proof** | *"Adding client case studies and logos provides critical **social proof**."* |

### B. Funnel Metrics, ROI & Analytics
| Basic Phrase (Avoid) | Executive Marketing Upgrade | How to Use It in a Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Cost to get 1 customer | **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)** | *"Rising ad prices pushed our **CAC** above \$200 last quarter."* |
| Money back from ads ("Cospa") | **Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) / ROI** | *"Webinar funnels deliver a much higher long-term **ROAS** than display ads."* |
| How much a buyer spends total | **Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)** | *"Our annual enterprise tier increased **Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)** by 45%."* |
| People who see the ad | **Impressions / Reach** | *"The video generated 300,000 **impressions**, but very few actual clicks."* |
| People who click the link | **Click-Through Rate (CTR)** | *"Testing a bolder headline boosted our **click-through rate (CTR)** from 1.2% to 3.4%."* |
| People leaving immediately | **Bounce rate / Funnel drop-off** | *"Slow mobile load times caused a massive 79% **bounce rate**."* |
| Customers canceling | **Customer churn (vs. Retention)** | *"Improving our onboarding sequence reduced quarterly **customer churn** by 18%."* |
| Knowing which ad worked | **Attribution** | *"The downside of podcast sponsorships is that direct **attribution** is difficult to track."* |
| Testing version A vs. B | **A/B testing / Split testing** | *"Let's **A/B test** two different landing page headlines this week."* |

### C. Campaign Strategy, Execution & Negotiations
| Basic Phrase (Avoid) | Executive Marketing Upgrade | How to Use It in a Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Plan to launch a product | **Go-To-Market (GTM) strategy** | *"We are finalizing our Q3 **Go-To-Market (GTM) strategy** for the Singapore launch."* |
| Get new potential buyers | **Drive lead generation** | *"Our primary objective this quarter is to **drive qualified lead generation**."* |
| Start working well / growing | **Gain traction** | *"After a slow first month, our referral campaign is finally **gaining traction**."* |
| Make a real difference | **Move the needle** | *"Minor color tweaks won't **move the needle** on our conversion rate."* |
| Easy wins first | **Low-hanging fruit** | *"Fixing our broken checkout link is the most obvious **low-hanging fruit**."* |
| Steal our own sales | **Cannibalize sales** | *"If we price the starter tier too low, it will **cannibalize** our Pro subscriptions."* |
| Cheap product to attract buyers | **Loss leader** | *"We use our \$10 starter audit as a **loss leader** to upsell consulting retainers."* |
| Move money from X to Y | **Allocate / Reallocate budget** | *"Let's **reallocate** \$20,000 from underperforming display ads into paid search."* |
| Redo / change ("Remake") | **Overhaul / Re-architect** | *"We need to completely **overhaul** our onboarding email sequence."* |
| Not in the contract ("NG") | **Out of scope (Scope creep)** | *"Adding three custom videos at this stage is **out of scope** for Phase 1."* |
