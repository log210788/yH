# Lesson 1: Teacher Lesson Plan
## The Executive Hook & Frame Control
### Commanding the First 90 Seconds, Eradicating Neediness & Capturing the "Croc Brain"

- **Module**: Selling, Pitching & Business Presentations (Lesson 1 of 4)
- **Student**: YH (Conversational B1–B2 ➔ Executive B2/C1)
- **Tutor**: Lewis O. | **Platform**: Preply
- **Duration**: 50 Minutes
- **Key Reference Text**: *Pitch Anything* by Oren Klaff (Adapting Frame Control & The Hook Point for ESL Executives)
- **Core Objective**: Teach YH how to open business presentations and sales pitches with unshakeable authority, eliminate apologetic hesitation (*"I'm sorry to bother you..."*), set the Time Frame, and deliver a magnetic 3-part hook within 90 seconds.

---

## 1. Lesson Objectives
By the end of this 50-minute session, YH will:
1. **Shatter the "Beta Trap"**: Replace apologetic, subservient openers with high-status executive peer framing.
2. **Execute the Downward Declarative Inflection**: Eliminate hesitant uptalk (rising pitch on statements) and finish sentences with grounded vocal authority.
3. **Deploy the 3-Part Hook Formula**: Deliver a compelling 60–90 second pitch opener comprising:
   - Part 1: *The Macro Shift* (What changed in the market/environment)
   - Part 2: *The High-Stakes Friction* (Why traditional methods are failing)
   - Part 3: *The Value Anchor* (How his solution uniquely resolves the tension)
4. **Enforce the Time Frame**: Confidently manage prospect time constraints without panicking (*"I actually have a hard stop at 25 past the hour..."*).
5. **Purge Katakana Traps**: Crisp consonant stops on *pitch*, *hook*, *product*, and *client* without trailing vowels.

## 3. Step-by-Step Facilitation & Exercise Bank

### Phase 1: Executive Warm-Up & Hook Diagnostic (00:00 – 08:00)
**Tutor Goal**: Wake up active English retrieval, break cognitive hesitation, and contrast weak "needy" openers with executive hooks.

#### Exercise 1.1: Weak vs. Executive Opener Rapid-Fire Callout (3 mins)
*Facilitation: Lewis reads a sentence aloud. YH has 2 seconds to call out **"NEEDY (Beta Trap)"** or **"EXECUTIVE (High Status)"**. If it is Needy, YH must immediately give an executive upgrade!*

1. *"Thank you so much for your precious time today, I know you are so busy so I will try not to take too long."*
   - **Diagnosis**: NEEDY. Subservient, apologetic, wastes time.
   - **YH Upgrade**: *"Thank you for prioritizing this discussion today. Let's dive right into the core agenda."*
2. *"Over the past six months, supply chain volatility has forced 40% of logistics teams to overhaul their forecasting."*
   - **Diagnosis**: EXECUTIVE. High-status, data-driven, establishes macro context.
3. *"I hope you like our slides, sorry my English is not so good today."*
   - **Diagnosis**: NEEDY. Catastrophic deal killer.
   - **YH Upgrade**: *"Our objective today is to share three high-impact findings that directly impact your operational efficiency."*
4. *"We have a lot of features in our tool, do you want me to show you everything?"*
   - **Diagnosis**: NEEDY / AIMLESS. Lacks leadership.
   - **YH Upgrade**: *"Based on your team's Q3 goals, I recommend focusing exclusively on two core capabilities."*

#### Exercise 1.2: The 30-Second Elevator Hook Sprint (5 mins)
*Facilitation: Give YH an everyday workplace trigger. He has 3 seconds to open with an intriguing hook:*
- **Trigger 1**: A new internal project management tool for your engineering team.
- **Trigger 2**: A proposal to automate client onboarding.
- **Rule**: No *"Ano..."*, no apologies. Must start with a bold statement or question.
- **Teacher Script**: *"YH, Trigger 1: Proposing an automated onboarding tool to your VP. 3... 2... 1... Hook me!"*

### Phase 2: Core Concept & Delivery Clinic (08:00 – 20:00)

#### A. The Pedagogy: The "Croc Brain" & Frame Control (Oren Klaff Method)
Explain this to YH in 60 seconds:
> *"YH, when you pitch, your prospect's brain doesn't listen with their rational neo-cortex first. They listen with their primitive 'Crocodile Brain'. The Croc Brain is easily bored, suspicious, and hates complexity. If you start with 20 bullet points or humble apologies, the Croc Brain falls asleep.*  
> *To wake up the buyer, you must use **Frame Control**: You must control the narrative frame. You are not a salesperson begging for money; you are a specialist doctor diagnosing a business problem."*

#### B. The 3-Part Executive Hook Formula
Walk YH through the formula:
```
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                  THE 3-PART EXECUTIVE HOOK FORMULA                                |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 1. THE MACRO SHIFT      | "Over the past 12-18 months, we've witnessed a dramatic shift in [X]..." |
| 2. THE TENSION / FRICTION| "Yet, most organizations are still relying on legacy approaches, leading |
|                         |  to [friction / wasted capital / operational bottleneck]..."             |
| 3. THE VALUE ANCHOR     | "Our objective today is to demonstrate how [our solution] eliminates      |
|                         |  this friction and drives [quantified business outcome]."                |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

#### C. Vocal Mechanics & Articulation Clinic
1. **The Downward Declarative Inflection (Banning "Uptalk")**:
   - Model the difference:
     - ❌ Uptalk (Insecure): *"We can reduce delivery cycle times by 30 percent? ↗"*
     - 💎 Downward Step (Authoritative): *"We can reduce delivery cycle times by **THIRTY PERCENT**. ↘"*
   - Have YH repeat 3 times, physically dropping his hand downward on the final word.
2. **The Consonant Guillotine (Clean Stops)**:
   - Drill these 4 words without trailing Katakana vowels:
     - *Pitch* (`/pɪtʃ/`) — NOT *pitch-u*
     - *Hook* (`/hʊk/`) — NOT *hook-u*
     - *Product* (`/ˈprɒd.ʌkt/`) — NOT *prod-uc-to*
     - *Client* (`/ˈklaɪ.ənt/`) — NOT *clien-to*

### Phase 3: 3-Tier Executive Lexical Upgrade (20:00 – 32:00)

Walk through the vocabulary ladder. Have YH read the Tier 3 phrase and immediately formulate a real workplace sentence:

| Elementary (Tier 1) | Professional (Tier 2) | Executive Impact (Tier 3) | Contextual Collocation & Usage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *I want to talk about* | *I'd like to present* | **I want to direct your attention to...** | Focuses executive attention immediately. |
| *We have a good tool* | *We built a strong solution* | **We have engineered a robust platform...** | High-status engineering & business credibility. |
| *Fix big problems* | *Solve major issues* | **Alleviate critical bottlenecks...** | Targets operational pain points. |
| *Make work faster* | *Speed up operations* | **Accelerate time-to-market...** | Classic executive value metric. |
| *Hope you like it* | *We think you will find this useful* | **This directly addresses your core mandate.** | Aligns with buyer's executive responsibilities. |
| *I think maybe* | *In my opinion* | **From our strategic vantage point...** | Demonstrates domain mastery and perspective. |

### Phase 4: Workplace Simulation Lab (32:00 – 45:00)
> **TEACHER ACTION**: **Turn OFF screen sharing now!** Direct webcam eye contact only.

#### Simulation Scenario 1: The "I Only Have 10 Minutes" Time Frame Test (6 mins)
- **Teacher Persona**: *Marcus Vance*, Chief Operating Officer. Rushed, checking watch, slightly abrupt.
- **Opening Line (Lewis)**: *"Hey YH, look—something just blew up with the board. I know we had 30 minutes scheduled, but I honestly only have 10 minutes. Can you just quickly give me the gist?"*
- **Target Student Behavior**:
  - Does NOT apologize or sound flustered.
  - Controls the Time Frame: *"Understood, Marcus. Actually, 10 minutes is plenty. In fact, I only need 7 minutes to show you the core numbers, leaving 3 minutes for next steps. Let’s jump straight in."*
  - Fires the 3-Part Hook immediately!

#### Simulation Scenario 2: The 90-Second Enterprise Client Pitch (7 mins)
- **Teacher Persona**: *Sarah Jenkins*, VP of Product at a multinational tech client.
- **Brief**: YH must deliver a full 90-second pitch opening his proposal for a new cross-functional workflow system.
- **Rubric Criteria**:
  - [ ] Zero apologies.
  - [ ] Crisp consonant endings (/t/, /k/, /tʃ/).
  - [ ] Downward declarative intonation.
  - [ ] Includes Macro Shift, Friction, and Value Anchor.
  - [ ] At least two Tier-3 collocations.
