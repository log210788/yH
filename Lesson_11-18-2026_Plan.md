## 15–40 min | Core Task: Executive Listening Tour & Consultative Pitch (70% STT)
**Two Back-to-Back Role-Plays (Lewis plays the Singapore staff member / prospective client; minimal teacher talk):**

- **Round 1 — Leadership 1-on-1 (12 min):**
  - *Prompt 1 (Skeptical Senior Staff):* "We've been doing things this way for years—why change now?" *(Honor the foundation + introduce the "next chapter".)*
  - *Prompt 2 (Overworked Employee):* "Our workload is too heavy because recruitment is frozen." *(Acknowledge, locate the bottleneck, prioritize together.)*
- **Round 2 — Consultative Pitch (13 min):**
  - *Prompt 3 (Discovery First):* Pitch your actual company service—or a B2B wellness/operations solution—**without** describing the product until you ask **2 situational discovery questions**.
  - *Prompt 4 (Objection Handling):* Lewis says, *"It sounds expensive and we're too busy to onboard."* Respond in under 45 seconds.

**Target Phrases:** `listening tour` · `North Star` · `still acclimatizing` · `my door is always open` · `bottleneck` · `if that sounds like a fit`

## 40–50 min | Live Silent-Scribe Debrief & Micro-Homework
- **40–47 min (Silent-Scribe Review):** Highlight 3 sharp executive moments + 2 real-time phrasing/pronunciation upgrades (focus on steady rhythm without Katakana/filler pauses).
- **47–50 min (Micro-Homework — Under 5 Min):** Write or record **3 situational discovery questions** you could ask a new client or team member in your first week (*"Have you ever run into a situation where…?"*).
