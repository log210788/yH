Exercise 1 • Adapted from Bolen's "Same Same but Different" (split_067)

The Asymmetrical Briefing Audit (100% Routine vs. 70/30 Foresight)

Compare Version A (a passive routine task update) with Version B (an authoritative 70/30 executive briefing). Identify the 5 strategic & lexical upgrades and click each target phrase in the Word Bank as you explain it to Louie.

Click to Check Off Target Upgrades Used in Your Audit:
I spearheaded a catalog audit dilute operational bandwidth onboarding high-margin SKUs half-goal circuit breaker anticipate latent risks information asymmetry

❌ Version A: Passive Routine Report (Creates Anxiety)

"Hi Boss, this month our team looked at the APAC sales data. We stopped spending time on useless things and put 30 new items from our good brands on the website. Sales went up 180% so far. Also, our new warehouse staff are still learning, and if some bad things happen next month during the holiday rush, we will tell you right away."

✅ Version B: The 70/30 Executive Brief (Eliminates Asymmetry)

"To update you on our APAC Q3 mandate: I spearheaded a catalog audit to ensure we don't dilute operational bandwidth across low-yield channels. By onboarding 30 high-margin SKUs from our top-performing brand partners, we have driven 180% YoY revenue growth. Looking ahead to Q4 (30% Risk Foresight): to prevent fulfillment bottlenecks with our newly onboarded warehouse team, I have installed a half-goal circuit breaker this Friday to audit packing velocity—and we have a backup 3PL partner on standby so Q4 delivery SLAs remain 100% protected."

👨‍🏫 Teacher Key (5 Upgrades): (1) Our team looked at → I spearheaded a catalog audit; (2) Useless things → dilute operational bandwidth across low-yield channels; (3) Put 30 new items → onboarding 30 high-margin SKUs; (4) Still learning → installed a half-goal circuit breaker; (5) If bad things happen → pre-engineered backup 3PL mitigation.
