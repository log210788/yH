## Part 1: Katakana & *Wasei-Eigo* Purge (Model Answers & Commentary)

1. *"Our client called us with a big claim because the system was down yesterday."*
   - **Model Answer**: *"Our client **lodged a major complaint** (or **raised an escalation**) because of yesterday's system downtime."*
   - **Tutor Notes**: In Japanese, クレーム (claim) means customer complaint. In English, a customer *lodges a complaint*, *raises an issue*, or *escalates a concern*. Ensure YH avoids saying *"made a claim"* unless referring to insurance.

2. *"We checked the software options, and this vendor has the best cospa for our team."*
   - **Model Answer**: *"We evaluated the software options, and this vendor offers the most **cost-effective solution** (or the **highest ROI / best value proposition**) for our team."*
   - **Tutor Notes**: コスパ (cospa / cost-performance) is Japanese business slang. Western/international counterparts use *cost-effective*, *high ROI*, or *strong value proposition*.

3. *"Can you take an appo with the regional director for next Thursday afternoon?"*
   - **Model Answer**: *"Could you **schedule a meeting** (or **lock in a sync / send a calendar invite**) with the regional director for next Thursday afternoon?"*
   - **Tutor Notes**: アポ (appo / appoint) cannot be used as a noun for a meeting. Acceptable verbs: *schedule a sync*, *set up a call*, *lock in a meeting*.

4. *"The design team needs to remake the entire presentation before the client meeting."*
   - **Model Answer**: *"The design team needs to **overhaul** (or **revise / redesign / re-architect**) the entire presentation before the client meeting."*
   - **Tutor Notes**: *Remake* is exclusively for media/films. Business documents are *overhauled, revised, reworked,* or *redesigned*.

5. *"Management said the proposed Q4 hiring budget is totally NG."*
   - **Model Answer**: *"Executive management stated that the proposed Q4 hiring budget is **completely non-viable** (or **out of scope / off the table / declined**)."*
   - **Tutor Notes**: NG (No Good) is widely used in Japan and parts of East Asia, but confuses Western stakeholders. *Non-viable*, *off the table*, or *disapproved* are standard executive terms.

6. *"Please send the contract to legal so we can get the sign before Friday."*
   - **Model Answer**: *"Please forward the contract to legal so we can secure **formal sign-off** (or **client signature / approval**) before Friday."*
   - **Tutor Notes**: *Sign* is a noun for a physical board or billboard; the document approval is a *signature* or *sign-off*.

## Part 2: The 3-Tier Business Vocabulary Upgrade Ladder (Model Answers)

1. *"We need to **make the process faster** so we don't waste time."*
   - **Model Answer**: *"We need to **streamline our workflows** to eliminate redundancies and operational friction."*
   - **Alternative**: *"We should **accelerate throughput** to optimize productivity."*

2. *"There is a **big problem** in our development timeline right now."*
   - **Model Answer**: *"We have encountered a **critical bottleneck** (or **major roadblock / core blocker**) in our development timeline."*
   - **Alternative**: *"A **substantial impediment** has emerged in our sprint schedule."*

3. *"We did this test early to **lower the bad risks** before the product launch."*
   - **Model Answer**: *"We conducted early testing to **mitigate potential deployment risks** ahead of the product launch."*
   - **Alternative**: *"We initiated preliminary audits to **mitigate downside exposure**."*

4. *"Let's have a quick call to **check that everyone agrees** on the goals."*
   - **Model Answer**: *"Let's schedule a brief sync to **align on expectations and core deliverables**."*
   - **Alternative**: *"Let's connect to ensure cross-functional teams are **fully aligned on priorities**."*

5. *"The main contract is ready, but we still need to **fix the small issues**."*
   - **Model Answer**: *"The primary agreement is finalized; we simply need to **iron out a few technical details and edge cases**."*
   - **Alternative**: *"We just need to **iron out the remaining kinks** before formal execution."*

6. *"I will **talk to you again** once I get the data from marketing."*
   - **Model Answer**: *"I will **circle back** (or **touch base**) with you as soon as marketing delivers the updated dataset."*
   - **Alternative**: *"I will **reconnect with you** once the figures are confirmed."*
