```

|                                 50-MINUTE LESSON FLOW ARCHITECTURE                                |

| 00:00 - 08:00    | 08:00 - 20:00     | 20:00 - 32:00     | 32:00 - 45:00      | 45:00 - 50:00     |
| Phase 1:         | Phase 2:          | Phase 3:          | Phase 4:           | Phase 5:          |
| Executive Warm-Up| Core Pitching     | 3-Tier Executive  | Live Workplace     | Cool-Down,        |
| & Anti-Trans     | Framework &       | Lexical Upgrade   | Simulation Lab     | Recast Matrix     |
| Sprint (Drills)  | Delivery Clinic   | (Power Phrases)   | (Roleplay Shark)   | & Homework Brief  |
+------------------+-------------------+-------------------+--------------------+-------------------+
```

### Golden Coaching Rules for Lewis on Preply:
1. **70% Student Talking Time (STT) / 30% Teacher Talking Time (TTT)**: Never lecture for >45 seconds.
2. **Camera Contact Switch**: Kill screen-sharing during Phase 4 (Simulation Lab) so the student looks directly into the webcam lens to pitch.
3. **Chat Box Subtitles**: Silently type mistakes and upgrades into the Preply chat while the student speaks: `❌ Old Phrasing ➔ 💎 Executive Upgrade`.
4. **Ban Apologies & Uptalk**: Enforce the downward declarative inflection on statements; freeze the student immediately if they open with *"I'm sorry..."*.
5. **Frame Elevation**: Never let the student play the desperate vendor ("beta trap"). Teach them to pitch as an equal strategic partner.
