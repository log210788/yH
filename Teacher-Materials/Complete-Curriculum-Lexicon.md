## Domain 1: 🤝 Meetings (Agile Syncs, Standups, Strategy Discussions)

| Term / Collocation | Part of Speech | Level 1 Basic Equivalent | Level 3 Executive Definition & Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Streamline operations** | Verb + Noun | Make work faster | *To eliminate redundancies and optimize process flow.* ("We streamlined the sign-off process.") |
| **Critical bottleneck** | Noun Phrase | Big delay point | *The primary constraint restricting project velocity.* ("QA testing is our critical bottleneck.") |
| **Align on expectations** | Verb + Prep | Agree on things | *To establish unanimous consensus on scope and timeline.* ("Let's align on deliverables before Friday.") |
| **Iron out the kinks** | Phrasal Verb | Fix small bugs | *To resolve minor operational discrepancies or friction.* ("We just need to iron out a few data formatting kinks.") |
| **Circle back / Touch base** | Phrasal Verb | Talk again later | *To reconnect briefly once fresh data is available.* ("I will circle back with you after the stakeholder review.") |
| **Drive consensus** | Verb + Noun | Get people to agree | *To actively facilitate agreement among conflicting viewpoints.* ("My goal is to drive consensus across engineering and sales.") |
| **Table the discussion** | Verb + Noun | Stop talking now | *To postpone an agenda item to a subsequent session.* ("Given time constraints, let's table that discussion until Tuesday.") |
| **Drill down into** | Phrasal Verb | Look deeper at | *To analyze granular specifics and root causes.* ("Let's drill down into the conversion metrics on slide 4.") |
| **Touch upon** | Phrasal Verb | Mention briefly | *To reference an issue without exhaustive analysis.* ("We touched upon regulatory compliance earlier.") |
| **Action item / Deliverable** | Noun Phrase | To-do task | *A concrete, assigned output with an explicit owner and deadline.* ("Let's confirm the action items before we wrap up.") |
| **Bandwidth** | Noun | Free time / capacity | *Cognitive or operational capacity to undertake work.* ("Our team doesn't have the bandwidth for extra sprints.") |
| **Pivot** | Verb / Noun | Change direction | *To strategically alter direction in response to market feedback.* ("We need to pivot toward mobile-first onboarding.") |
| **Move the needle** | Idiom | Make a big difference | *To generate a noticeable, measurable impact.* ("This feature update will genuinely move the needle on retention.") |
| **Take offline** | Verb Phrase | Talk privately | *To discuss a complex topic outside the general meeting.* ("Let's take the architecture debate offline.") |
