READING: RETAIL INSIDER CASE STUDY
-----------------------------------------------------

From Orchard Road to Everyone's Basket: How Don Don Donki Made Japan Feel Local in Singapore

Singapore's retail market is crowded, expensive and fiercely competitive. Rents on Orchard Road are among the highest in Asia, online shopping is only a tap away, and customers switch brands easily. Yet when Japanese discount chain Don Don Donki opened its first Singapore store at Orchard Central in December 2017, shoppers lined up to get in.

The secret was entertainment. Rather than behaving like a quiet importer, Donki turned shopping into a treasure hunt. Its famous "jungle" layout stacks products from floor to ceiling in winding aisles, so customers keep browsing and discover items they never planned to buy. The store launched with round-the-clock opening hours and sells everything from fresh sushi and grilled skewers to snacks, skincare and kitchenware. A catchy theme song and a penguin mascot make the brand instantly recognizable.

Just as important, Donki does not rely only on imports. It also sells its own private-label products, which protect its profit margins and give customers a reason to come back. After the success on Orchard Road, the chain expanded into neighborhood malls, bringing "a little bit of Japan" closer to everyday shoppers.

The lesson for food brands: sell an experience, not just a product. Localize your offer, but keep the authentic Japanese appeal that people are willing to pay for.

Key words: entertainment retail - jungle layout - private label - margin - impulse purchase - localize
