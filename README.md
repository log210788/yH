## 🎯 Baseline Assessment: Natural Delivery First

To establish a true baseline of YH's natural communication style, the prompts are intentionally open and free of rigid scripts or formulaic templates. Each option provides a realistic client scenario with a **gentle nudge** (simple thought-starters) so YH can speak naturally and authentically.

---

## 🎙️ Practice Options Available Today

YH can choose any of the following 5 client presentation scenarios to practice and record:

1. **Option 1: The 3-Minute Product Value Pitch (New Client / Prospect)**
   - *Scenario:* Meeting a prospective client for an introductory chat. Pitch your product in your own words, focusing on what it does and why it makes their life easier.
   - *A Gentle Nudge:* What problem does the client usually have? How does your product solve it or save them time? What would you invite them to do next?
2. **Option 2: Live Feature Walkthrough & Solution Demo**
   - *Scenario:* Screensharing with a client to walk them through one or two key features of your product.
   - *A Gentle Nudge:* Which feature are you showing and why is it useful? How does someone use it in practice? Ask the client a question to check their thoughts!
3. **Option 3: Handling Tough Client Objections (Cost, Switching & Timing)**
   - *Scenario:* Answering concerns like *"Your solution looks great, but switching sounds risky and our budget is tight this quarter."*
   - *A Gentle Nudge:* Acknowledge their concern calmly. Explain why it's worth it in the long run. Propose a simple, safe next step.
4. **Option 4: Client Success Review & Expansion / Upsell**
   - *Scenario:* Catching up with an existing client to review how things are going and mention another feature or team that could benefit.
   - *A Gentle Nudge:* Mention positive results so far. Bring up another feature or team that could benefit. Ask if they'd be open to exploring it.
5. **Option 5: Custom Work Pitch (Present His Actual Real-World Product)**
   - *Scenario:* Present his real company product or demo just like on an actual client call.
   - *A Gentle Nudge:* Speak freely in your own words—no scripts needed!

## 🎧 Step-by-Step Guide for YH

1. **Pick an Option:** Open [https://log210788.github.io/yH/](https://log210788.github.io/yH/) and select any scenario.
2. **Review the Nudge:** Read the quick thought-starters (and optional vocabulary chips if helpful).
3. **Record in Browser (2–3 Minutes):** Click **"Start Recording"** (allow microphone). Speak naturally.
4. **Listen Back & Download:** Preview your take. When happy, click **"Download Recording"** to save your audio file (e.g. `YH_Client_ProductPitch_2026-09-22.webm`).
5. **Send in Preply:** Drop the downloaded audio file directly into your **Preply Messages / Classroom** for your tutor's review before next lesson!
