# YH — Presentation & Communication Studio

A minimalist web portal and in-browser audio recording suite designed for **YH** to practice workplace presentations, master sentence stress and natural rhythm, and prepare for his **4-Week Milestone Assessment** in his Preply English coaching sessions.

🌐 **Live GitHub Pages URL:** [https://log210788.github.io/yH/](https://log210788.github.io/yH/)

---

## 🎯 3 Core Sections

1. **🎙️ Recording Studio (Today's Practice):** Choose a presentation topic, read a gentle nudge (thought-starters), record your voice in the browser with live visualizer & timer, listen back, and download the audio file.
2. **⚡ Cadence & Rhythm Lab:** Interactive exercises directly practicing what was covered in class:
   - **60 BPM Isochrony Metronome Drill:** 3-beat cadence retention (*"CATS EAT MICE"*).
   - **Subtext Navigation Drill:** How shifting sentence stress completely changes the underlying workplace message (*"I didn't tell him to cancel the project"*).
   - **Two-Syllable Business Word Stress Diagnostic:** 1st syllable noun stress vs. 2nd syllable verb stress (*REC-ord vs. re-CORD*, *PRES-ent vs. pre-SENT*, *PROJ-ect vs. pro-JECT*).
3. **🎯 4-Week Milestone Assessment:** The 100-point benchmark rubric evaluating development across 4 weeks.

---

## 🎙️ Presentation Topics Available

- **Topic 1: 3-Minute Product Overview (Pitch to Client)** — Pitch your product in your own words, focusing on what it does and why it makes their life easier.
- **Topic 2: Live Feature Walkthrough & Demo** — Screenshare and walk through 1–2 key features and how they are used.
- **Topic 3: Addressing Client Concerns & Questions** — Answering client doubts (cost, switching risk, timing) with calm reassurance.
- **Topic 4: Client Review & Project Update** — Reviewing milestones and positive results with an existing client.
- **Topic 5: Your Real Work Product Presentation** — Pitch your actual real-world product or demo freely.

## 🎧 Step-by-Step Guide for YH

1. **Pick a Topic:** Open [https://log210788.github.io/yH/](https://log210788.github.io/yH/) and select a topic from the dropdown.
2. **Read the Nudge:** Think through the 2–3 gentle thought-starters (use the optional Speaker Notes box if you want to jot down key points).
3. **Record in Browser (2–3 Minutes):** Click **"Start Recording"** (allow microphone). Speak naturally.
4. **Listen Back & Download:** Preview your take. When happy, click **"Download Recording"** to save your audio file (e.g. `YH_Product_Overview_2026-09-22.webm`).
5. **Send in Preply:** Drop the downloaded audio file directly into your **Preply Messages / Classroom** for your tutor's review!
