Activity 7 · Lexical Precision & Paraphrasing

Plain-English to Executive Marketing Upgrade

Adapted from: Sentence Substitution Ladder (p. 218) & Paraphrasing (p. 133)
Interactive Substitution Ladder
Base Sentence: "We want to get more customers by changing our website button."
Rung 1 (Verb + Noun): "We want to drive lead generation by changing our website button."
Rung 2 (Action Verb): "We want to drive lead generation by overhauling our website button."
Rung 3 (Full Executive Register): "We aim to drive qualified lead generation by overhauling our landing page call-to-action (CTA)."

1: "This campaign has an unsustainable CAC and is failing to move the needle on quarterly revenue."

2: "If we slash our pricing by 30%, we will erode our brand equity and cannibalize our Pro tier subscriptions."

3: "Instead of building a complex new funnel, let's capture the low-hanging fruit by optimizing our mobile bounce rate."

4: "In Q2, we allocated $20,000 to Instagram campaigns, generating 50,000 impressions but minimal conversions due to a page-latency bottleneck. For Q3, let's reallocate that budget into automated nurture sequences to boost client retention."

Activity 9 · Strategic Hypothesis & Case Reading

Business & Marketing Headline Prediction Lab (5Ws + H)

Adapted from: Headline Prediction Practice (pp. 129–130)
Case Study Headline #1

"Why B2B SaaS Company 'MetricFlow' Deleted Its Free Plan—And Saw Paid Conversions Jump 42%"

What Happened: MetricFlow found that 95% of its support bandwidth was consumed by free-tier users who never upgraded. Worse, the restricted free plan hid the product's core value proposition. By replacing the free tier with a 14-day full-featured trial + guided onboarding, they filtered out freebie-hunters, cut support overhead by 60%, and lifted paid conversion rates by 42%.
Case Study Headline #2

"How a Tokyo Coffee Chain Cut Ad Spend to $0 and Doubled Customer Retention in 6 Months"

What Happened: Instead of spending $30k/month on social ads with rising CAC and weak attribution, the chain reallocated its budget into an app-based Loss Leader Coffee Subscription ("1st month $15, bring a coworker free on Fridays"). Existing subscribers became a zero-CAC referral engine, doubling retention and Customer Lifetime Value (LTV).
